# Hi, I'm Sushma Kallu 👋

I lead engineering teams that connect **Microsoft 365 Copilot** to the world's business data.

As a **Principal Software Engineering Manager at Microsoft** in Bengaluru, I work on **Copilot Connectors**, the extensibility layer that lets organizations and ISVs bring their own content into Copilot securely, compliantly and at scale. Our goal is simple: customers should get the same relevance, quality and trust from Copilot no matter where their data lives.

---

### 🚀 What I work on
- 🔌 **Copilot Connectors** – a unified experience across out-of-the-box and custom connectors
- 🧱 **Data quality** – schema validation, document parsing and indexing at scale
- 🎯 **Relevance** – making custom data as discoverable as first-party content
- 🔐 **Security & compliance** – sensitivity labels and information protection for connector content
- 📊 **Observability** – metrics for ingestion, index freshness and completeness
- 🧪 **Developer experience** – self-serve onboarding and automated evaluation for connector builders

### 🌱 Currently learning and building
- AI-assisted engineering with **GitHub Copilot** and agent skills
- **Microservices on Azure** – Python, FastAPI and Azure Container Apps
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and enterprise search
- Automated evaluation of AI answer quality

### 💡 How I lead
I believe great engineering is equal parts **curiosity, accountability and kindness**.
I care about ownership, technical depth and learning out loud, through knowledge-sharing sessions, thoughtful code reviews and making room for engineers to experiment with AI.

I'm also a **Diversity & Inclusion Champion** for my organization and love mentoring engineers.
